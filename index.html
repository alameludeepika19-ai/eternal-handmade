Handmade Soy & Scented Candles in Coimbatore

Welcome to Eternal Handmade, your destination for beautifully handcrafted scented candles in Coimbatore. Established in 2022, we specialize in creating premium coconut soy candles in Coimbatore using pre tested combination of natural waxes, carefully selected fragrances, and elegant handcrafted designs.

Whether you're looking for personalized return gifts, festive décor, birthday candles, wedding favors, luxury hampers, or unique home décor, every candle is made with attention to detail. Each order is customized to match your style, theme, fragrance, colors, and special occasion, making every creation truly one of a kind.

From small gifts to bulk event orders, our mission is to bring warmth, fragrance, and happiness into every home through handcrafted candles made with love.
