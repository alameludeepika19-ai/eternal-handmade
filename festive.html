Diwali Season – Order Information

As we prepare for the festive season, we request our customers to place their Diwali orders in advance to ensure timely processing and delivery.

Order Processing

All orders are processed on a first come, first serve basis. We recommend placing your orders early during the festive season.

Dispatch Timeline

Orders require approximately 8–10 days for dispatch after order confirmation.

Delivery Timeline

For locations outside Coimbatore, please allow an additional 2–3 days for delivery after dispatch.

Coimbatore Pickup

We are based in Coimbatore and provide a pickup option from our place for customers who prefer to collect their orders.

Customisation Available

Customisation is available for selected products. You can discuss your preferred colours, fragrances, sizes and decorations with us.

Urgent Orders & Enquiries

For urgent orders or enquiries regarding your Diwali requirements, please contact us directly through WhatsApp.

Special Diwali Offer: Get 16% OFF on orders above 15 pieces.

Kindly plan your orders in advance considering the processing and delivery timelines.
